title: Emoji & Symbols
description: Search emoji and symbols by name and paste them straight into whatever you were typing.

![Emoji & Symbols](/openray/screenshots/emoji.svg)

**Search Emoji & Symbols** opens a grid you search by Unicode name — `rocket`, `arrow`,
`euro`. ↵ pastes into the app you came from; **Copy Emoji** puts it on the clipboard instead.

The last 20 you used lead the grid under **Recently Used**, and the dropdown filters by
category: Smileys & Emotion, Symbols & Pictographs, Transport & Places, Supplemental,
Extended, Miscellaneous, Dingbats, Arrows, Currency.

The dataset is bundled, so search works offline and is identical on every platform.
Skin-tone variants and combined sequences such as flags are not included.

Works on macOS, Windows and Linux.
